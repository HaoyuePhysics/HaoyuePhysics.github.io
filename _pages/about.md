I am **Haoyue Jiang**, a graduate researcher at **UC Berkeley**, affiliated with **Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory**.

My research combines photoemission spectroscopy, ultrafast optics, and scientific instrumentation to investigate the electronic structure of quantum materials. I am interested in both the physics we can measure and the instruments that make those measurements possible.

## Research

### Photoemission and quantum materials

I use angle-resolved photoemission spectroscopy (ARPES) to investigate the electronic structure of quantum materials, with a particular interest in cuprate superconductors.

### Ultrafast spectroscopy

I work on high-harmonic-generation (HHG) sources and time-resolved ARPES instrumentation, connecting ultrafast optical techniques with measurements of electronic structure.

### X-ray optics and interferometry

My instrumentation work includes the development of Fourier-transform X-ray spectroscopy (FTXR), with a focus on interferometry, optical alignment, experimental control, and data analysis.
