<header class="page-intro"><p class="eyebrow">Experimental physics</p><h1>Optical &amp; spectroscopic<br class="desktop-break"> instrumentation</h1><p class="lead">I develop optical and spectroscopic instrumentation, experimental software, and measurement workflows.</p></header>
<p>I am <strong>Haoyue Jiang</strong>, a PhD student in <strong>Applied Science and Technology at UC Berkeley</strong> and a graduate researcher in <strong>Alessandra Lanzara’s Photoemission Lab</strong>, working at UC Berkeley and Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory.</p>
<p>My current work focuses on the HARMONY 2.0 HHG / tr-ARPES platform, a Fourier-transform interferometer for future soft X-ray spectroscopy, and cuprate research with ARPES and RIXS.</p>
