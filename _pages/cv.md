<h2>Research experience</h2>
<h3>Graduate Student Researcher · September 2023–present</h3>
<p><strong>Photoemission Lab, UC Berkeley / Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory</strong><br/>
Advisor: Prof. Alessandra Lanzara</p>
<p><strong>HHG / tr-ARPES platform development.</strong> Independently redesigned and rebuilt the HHG optical system from an 800 nm configuration to a 1030 nm CARBIDE-based setup. Designed optical paths, selected all optical components, calculated nonlinear-conversion parameters and filter thicknesses, machined custom optomechanical hardware, and performed assembly, alignment, and debugging.</p>
<p><strong>New optical capabilities.</strong> Work includes probe focusing and spot-size reduction and pump-beam development at multiple wavelengths. OPA and circularly polarized probe capabilities are in design.</p>
<p><strong>Experimental software and analysis.</strong> Develop sample-management systems, experimental software, and operation, acquisition, and analysis workflows. Ongoing team projects include a PyARPES-based tr-ARPES UI, ChatARPES (LLM), and clustering of time-resolved data.</p>
<p><strong>Soft X-ray Fourier-transform interferometry.</strong> Develop and characterize an interferometer toward soft X-ray spectroscopy at ALS-U. Current work combines visible-light validation, scan control, displacement metrology, and spectral reconstruction.</p>
<p><strong>Cuprate research.</strong> Ongoing ARPES/RIXS research on cuprates. ARPES work examines band dispersions, Fermi surfaces, and spectral weight.</p>

<h3>Student Research Assistant · May 2019–November 2022</h3>
<p><strong>Photon Spectroscopy Lab, William &amp; Mary</strong><br/>
Advisor: Prof. Mumtaz Qazilbash</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Near-field infrared nanospectroscopy of SrTiO₃.</strong> Developed Green's-function and Drude models of experimental near-field amplitude and phase spectra for insulating bulk SrTiO₃ and metallic SrTiO₃ surfaces.</li>
<li><strong>Surface phonon-polariton propagation.</strong> Refined simulations of experimental geometries in the far infrared; calculated propagation wavelengths in SrTiO₃ and compared them with experiment.</li>
<li><strong>Numerical methods for optical materials.</strong> Built dipole-array models of finite beam-spot effects. Automated single-frequency FEKO simulations of one- and two-dimensional spatial scans and developed thickness-dependent optical models.</li>
<li><strong>Optical characterization of anisotropic Ti₂O₃ films.</strong> Measured X-ray reflectivity, ellipsometry (4,800–52,000 cm⁻¹), and infrared reflectivity (100–6,500 cm⁻¹). Fit experimental spectra with anisotropic Fresnel models.</li>
<li><strong>Microplastics spectroscopy.</strong> Measured far-field and near-field infrared spectra of marine microplastic samples.</li>
</ul>
<h3>Student Researcher · January 2015–May 2018</h3>
<p><strong>Thin Film Growth Lab, Southern University of Science and Technology</strong><br/>
Advisor: Prof. Lang Chen</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Magnetic tunnel junction growth and fabrication.</strong> Grew Cr₁₋δTe/ZnSe/Cr₁₋δTe heterostructures by molecular beam epitaxy (MBE). Patterned junctions using UV lithography and Ar ion etching, and investigated the layers' four-fold magnetic anisotropy.</li>
<li><strong>Strained multiferroic BiFeO₃ films.</strong> Grew epitaxial films of different thicknesses on (001)-oriented LaAlO₃ substrates by pulsed laser deposition (PLD). Used AFM and RSM-XRD to characterize structure and lattice parameters, and investigated the temperature-dependent transition from positive to negative thermal expansion.</li>
<li><strong>Biological piezoelectricity.</strong> Investigated the nanoscale morphology and piezoelectric response of green carp scales and the role of surface hydroxyapatite. First author of the resulting 2017 AIP Advances paper.</li>
</ul>
