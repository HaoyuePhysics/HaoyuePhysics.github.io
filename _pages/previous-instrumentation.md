<section id="infrared-optics"><h2>Near-field infrared nanoscopy and electromagnetic modeling</h2>
<p class="project-context">Photon Spectroscopy Lab, William &amp; Mary · May 2019–November 2022<br>Advisor: Prof. Mumtaz Qazilbash</p>
<h3>Near-field infrared response of insulating and metallic SrTiO₃</h3>
<p>I developed numerical methods to model experimental amplitude and phase spectra in scattering-type near-field infrared nanospectroscopy. I used Green’s-function and Drude-model approaches to describe insulating bulk SrTiO₃ and the response of metallic SrTiO₃ surfaces.</p>
<h3>Far-infrared surface phonon-polariton propagation</h3>
<p>I refined simulation models to represent experimental geometries for surface phonon-polariton propagation in SrTiO₃. I calculated propagation wavelengths and compared them with experimental measurements.</p>
<h3>Electromagnetic simulations and finite beam-spot effects</h3>
<p>I built dipole-array models to account for the finite spot size of experimental beams. I wrote scripts to automate FEKO simulations of one- and two-dimensional spatial scans at a single frequency, and developed models of thickness-dependent optical response.</p>
