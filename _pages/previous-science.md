<section id="thin-films-devices"><h2>Epitaxial thin-film growth &amp; device fabrication</h2>
<p class="project-context">Thin Film Growth Lab, SUSTech · January 2015–May 2018<br>Advisor: Prof. Lang Chen</p>
<h3>MBE growth and fabrication of magnetic tunnel junctions</h3>
<p>I grew Cr₁₋δTe/ZnSe/Cr₁₋δTe heterostructures by <strong>molecular beam epitaxy (MBE)</strong> and patterned magnetic tunnel junctions using <strong>UV lithography and Ar ion etching</strong>. I investigated the magnetic properties of the layers, including their four-fold anisotropy.</p>
<h3>PLD growth of highly strained multiferroic BiFeO₃ films</h3>
<p>I grew epitaxial BiFeO₃ films of different thicknesses on (001)-oriented LaAlO₃ substrates by <strong>pulsed laser deposition (PLD)</strong>. I used AFM and reciprocal-space mapping by X-ray diffraction (RSM-XRD) to characterize the films’ structure and c-axis lattice parameter. The project investigated a transition from positive to negative thermal expansion with increasing temperature in highly strained films.</p>
<h3>PLD-grown oxide superlattices and polar-vortex research</h3>
<p class="project-context">Interface Science Group, UC Irvine · June–September 2017<br>Advisor: Prof. Xiaoqing Pan</p>
<p>During a research visit to UC Irvine, I worked on polar vortices in (PbTiO₃)ₙ/(SrTiO₃)ₙ oxide superlattices. I grew the superlattices by PLD with n = 5, 10, and 30, and used scanning probe microscopy (SPM) and X-ray diffraction (XRD) to characterize morphology, nanoscale electrical features, and structure.</p>

<h3>Biological piezoelectricity</h3>
<p>At SUSTech, I also investigated the nanoscale morphology and piezoelectric response of green carp scales, including the role of surface hydroxyapatite. This work led to my first-author paper, <a href="{{ '/publications/#article-1' | relative_url }}">“Piezoelectricity of green carp scales”</a>.</p>
</section>
<section class="content-section" id="thin-film-optics"><h2>Thin-film optics &amp; infrared spectroscopy</h2>
<p class="project-context">Photon Spectroscopy Lab, William &amp; Mary · May 2019–November 2022<br>Advisor: Prof. Mumtaz Qazilbash</p>
<h3>Optical properties of anisotropic Ti₂O₃ films</h3>
<p>I measured X-ray reflectivity, ellipsometry, and infrared reflectivity of Ti₂O₃ films. I developed anisotropic optical models based on Fresnel equations to fit the experimental spectra. The measurements covered 4,800–52,000 cm⁻¹ in ellipsometry and 100–6,500 cm⁻¹ in infrared reflectivity.</p>
<p><a href="{{ '/publications/#article-3' | relative_url }}">Related publication: Electronic and optical properties of strain-locked metallic Ti₂O₃ films.</a></p>
<h3>Far-field and near-field infrared spectroscopy of microplastics</h3>
<p>I measured both far-field and near-field infrared spectra of microplastics collected from marine pollution samples.</p>
<p>Related modeling projects are presented under <a href="{{ '/research/previous-instrumentation/#infrared-optics' | relative_url }}">infrared optics and method development</a>.</p>
</section>
