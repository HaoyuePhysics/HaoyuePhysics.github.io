<header class="page-intro"><p class="eyebrow">Ongoing / HARMONY 2.0</p><h1>HHG / tr-ARPES<br class="desktop-break"> platform development</h1><p class="lead">Independent optical redesign and implementation, with continued development of experimental capabilities, scientific software, and analysis methods.</p></header>
<div class="project-context"><span>UC Berkeley / Berkeley Lab</span><span>Photoemission Lab</span></div>
<p>I independently redesigned and rebuilt the HHG optical system for our time-resolved ARPES platform, transitioning from the previous <strong>800 nm fundamental-wavelength configuration</strong> to a system based on a <strong>1030 nm CARBIDE laser</strong>.</p>
<p>I designed the new beam paths, selected all optical components, calculated nonlinear-conversion parameters and efficiencies—including BBO-based frequency conversion—and determined filter thicknesses for the revised configuration. I also <strong>machined custom optomechanical hardware</strong> and carried out the assembly, alignment, and debugging of the redesigned system.</p>

<section class="content-section" id="new-capabilities"><h2>Expanding pump–probe capabilities</h2>
<p>Building on the completed upgrade, my work includes probe focusing and spot-size reduction, together with pump-beam development at multiple wavelengths.</p>
<div class="design-note"><span class="status">In design</span><p>Optical parametric amplifier (OPA) capabilities and a circularly polarized probe.</p></div>
</section>
<section class="content-section" id="workflows"><h2>Experimental software &amp; workflows</h2>
<p>I develop sample-management systems, experimental software, and workflows for experimental operation, data acquisition, and analysis. This work includes sample tracking, experiment metadata, and instrument-use and maintenance scheduling.</p>
<p>These software and workflow developments are part of the experimental platform, alongside its optical hardware.</p>
</section>
