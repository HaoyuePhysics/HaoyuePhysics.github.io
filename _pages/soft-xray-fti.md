<header class="page-intro"><p class="eyebrow">Ongoing / Visible-light testing and characterization</p><h1>Fourier-Transform Interferometry for Soft X-ray Spectroscopy</h1></header>
<p>I am developing and characterizing a Fourier-transform interferometer toward soft X-ray spectroscopy applications at ALS-U. My work combines visible-light alignment, scan control, displacement metrology, and computational spectral reconstruction.</p>
<p>Visible-light measurements have demonstrated interference fringes and preliminary spectral reconstruction from broadband and laser sources. I have developed acquisition and analysis tools that connect camera images with measured stage positions to reconstruct interferograms and spectra. Current work focuses on quantifying repeatability and understanding how scan motion, alignment, and optical coherence affect the measured spectral linewidth.</p>
<p>The next phase is to establish a calibrated performance baseline and develop an experimentally constrained model to guide improvements and evaluate future soft X-ray performance.</p>
